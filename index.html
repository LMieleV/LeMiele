The five pillars 01 / 06

One letter, one file, one home for everything.

Every thought you hand the vault lands in exactly one of five files. No folders to invent, no app to learn — click a pillar to open its file.

How it operates 02 / 06

Three moves. No apps, no forms.

The vault runs on conversation. You never open a file to write — you talk, it files.

— 1

Say it

Talk normally in any session: capture: call the supplier, done: send the report, log achievement: … — or just ask a question and it answers from what you already know.

— 2

It's filed

The vault writes it into the right pillar, cross-links it to related pages, and appends a line to the history log. Nothing falls through.

— 3

Review weekly

Say weekly review — inbox to zero, goals checked for a next action, wins celebrated, reading queue fed, and a lint pass over the whole vault.

    Cheat sheet 06 / 06

    Six phrases run the whole vault.

    — 1capture: <anything>+

    Lands in the Tasks inbox untriaged — capture first, organize later, never lose an item because filing felt like work. Lives in .

    — 2done: <item>+

    The item is checked off and dated. Milestones are promoted to Achievements automatically — shipped work never disappears into a checkbox.

    — 3log achievement: <win>+

    Filed under the current month in with what happened, why it mattered, and links — ready for the next performance review or bad day.

    — 4log decision: <choice>+

    Recorded in with context, options, and rationale. History is never rewritten — a changed mind adds a new entry and supersedes the old one.

    — 5ingest raw/<file>+

    The source's key points become a knowledge page, catalogued under Understanding in . Sources in raw/ are immutable — the vault reads, never edits.

    — 6weekly review+

    The full ritual: inbox to zero, every active goal checked for a next action, the week's wins celebrated, the reading queue fed, and a lint pass for contradictions and broken links.
