## Kill criteria (pre-registered, to stay honest)

- If after 9 months of founder-led selling < 5 entities have paid anything → the
  mid-market thesis is wrong; pivot to the consultancy-tooling channel exclusively or stop.
- If AI drafting can't hit ≥80% analyst-accepted-without-major-edit rate on design-partner
  files by month 6 → wedge is premature; fall back to workflow + evidence room (still
  sellable, lower price point) while models improve.
