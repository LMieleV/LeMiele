# Funding Roadmap

## Principle

Capital-efficient EU path, de-risked with non-dilutive money first (the Luxembourg
ecosystem is unusually generous to exactly this profile of company), venture capital
when the metrics make it cheap.

## Stage 0 — Non-dilutive: Fit4Start (target: next cohort)

- **What**: Luxinnovation/Ministry of the Economy accelerator; up to **€150k grant**
  (tranches €50k/€80k/€20k) + 6 months coaching; ~20 startups selected per cohort
  from 400+ applications; digital/fintech category fits Kloer exactly.
- **Requirements**: incorporation in Luxembourg (SARL-S suffices), <5 years old,
  early-stage.
- **Follow-on**: startups that raise and hire 2+ employees may access **+€100k**.
- Also stack: **young innovative enterprise aid** and R&D aid (RDI scheme) via the
  Ministry of the Economy for the AI pipeline development.

## Stage 1 — Pre-seed: €750k at month ~6–9

- **Sources**: LBAN (Luxembourg Business Angel Network) syndicate, LHoFT-adjacent
  angels (ex-fund-industry operators understand the pain viscerally and are the
  ideal cap-table members — they're also pipeline), Digital Tech Fund
  (state-backed VC, invests pre-seed/seed in LU tech), plus one EU pre-seed fund
  (Expon, Mangrove, Kima-style) for signal.
- **Milestones to raise on**: 3 design partners live, wedge metric proven
  (€2,500 → €400/file), Fit4Start badge.
- **Use**: 4 hires (2 eng, 1 compliance expert, 1 CS), 18 months runway with grant.

## Stage 2 — Seed: €3M at month ~18–21

- **Milestones**: €1M ARR run-rate, NRR >110%, consultancy channel signed, ISO 27001
  in progress, Ireland module scoped.
- **Sources**: EU B2B SaaS seed funds + strategic angle (Big-4-affiliated CVCs, fund
  administrator strategics — take money from at most one to avoid channel conflict).
- **Use**: scale to 26 FTE, Ireland launch, marketplace module.

## Stage 3 — Series A: month ~34+ (optional)

At €4M ARR growing >100% with 82% gross margin and near-break-even economics, Series A
is a choice, not a need — the deliberate position (profitably-default-alive is a
stronger negotiating stance in the EU market than growth-at-all-costs).

## Dilution model

| Round | Amount | Post-money | Dilution |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fit4Start + grants | €250k | — | 0% |
| Pre-seed | €750k | €4M | ~19% |
| Seed | €3M | €14M | ~21% |
| Founders + ESOP at seed | | | Founders ~55%, ESOP 10% |
