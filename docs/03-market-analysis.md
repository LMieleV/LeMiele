# Market Analysis — Luxembourg

## 1. The macro picture

Luxembourg is a 680k-person country with an outsized financial sector:

- **~€7.4 trillion** in fund assets under management (June 2025) — Europe's #1,
  world's #2 fund domicile after the US.
- Thousands of regulated vehicles (UCITS, Part II funds, SIFs, RAIFs, SICARs) served
  by hundreds of ManCos, AIFMs, fund administrators, transfer agents and depositaries.
- ~120+ banks, ~280 PSF (professionals of the financial sector), a large insurance
  sector (CAA-regulated), plus the crypto/VASP register — all AML-obligated entities.
- Beyond finance: every notary, real-estate agency, law firm and accounting firm is an
  AML-obligated "professional" under the 2004 AML law — a long-tail second market.

## 2. The forcing functions (why budgets exist)

1. **CSSF 2026 supervisory priorities** — AML/CFT remains a core priority; increased
   scrutiny of governance, third-party risk, cyber resilience (DORA), liquidity,
   valuation and investor costs. On-site inspections and questionnaires (the annual
   AML/CFT survey) keep rising in depth.
2. **DORA** (applies since Jan 2025) — digital operational resilience obligations,
   including register of ICT third parties: a compliance-data problem crying for software.
3. **EU AML package** — AMLR + AMLA (the new EU AML Authority) phases in 2026-2027 with
   directly applicable rules and a single rulebook: every entity must re-map controls.
4. **Cost pressure** — fund fees compress; back-office compliance cost is the CFO's
   target line item. KYC review at $1,500–3,000/file × hundreds of investor and
   counterparty files per entity per year is the visible bleed.

## 3. TAM / SAM / SOM

| Layer | Definition | Count (est.) | ACV | Value |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| TAM | All Luxembourg AML-obligated entities + Ireland expansion | ~10,000 | €12k avg | ~€120M/yr |
| SAM | Luxembourg fund-industry entities (ManCos, AIFMs, admins, TAs, depositaries, PSF support) | ~2,500–3,000 | €20k avg | ~€55M/yr |
| SOM (36 months) | Mid-market fund entities won via direct + partner channel | 150 | €26k avg | ~€3.9M ARR |

The SAM is small by US standards but **dense, legally compelled to buy, and
geographically concentrated** — a textbook beachhead (see ServiceTitan analysis in
`02-us-playbook.md`). Ireland (#2 EU fund domicile, English-speaking, similar rulebook
under CBI) roughly doubles SAM with <20% product change.

## 4. Competitive landscape

| Player | What they sell | Weakness Kloer exploits |
|---|---|---|
| **Finologee** (KYC Manager) | KYC lifecycle workflow platform; regulated PFS; KPMG channel partnership (2025) | Enterprise-first, services-heavy deployments, not AI-native; mid-market underserved |
| **KYC3** | AI risk screening, relationship mapping, monitoring | Point solution (screening), not the full compliance operating system |
| **Governance.com** | Data/entity governance and compliance workflow for funds | Workflow/data tool; periodic not continuous; limited AI drafting |
| **Big-4 / consultants** | Manual KYC remediation, RC-as-a-service | €150–300/hr; Kloer turns them into a channel (their staff work *in* Kloer) |
| **Horizontal GRC (ServiceNow, LogicGate…)** | Generic GRC | No CSSF rulebook depth, no local credibility, enterprise pricing |
| **US trust-management clones (Vanta, Drata)** | SOC2/ISO frameworks | Don't cover CSSF/AML/fund-specific obligations at all — validates the model, doesn't compete in it |

**Positioning:** *AI-native continuous compliance for the fund industry* — vs.
workflow tools (incumbents) and vs. consultants (the real competitor). The wedge
feature is the **AI-drafted KYC review file** at ~85% time reduction, because it
attacks the single largest measurable cost.

## 6. Ecosystem tailwinds

- **LHoFT** (Luxembourg House of Financial Technology): direct access to every bank
  and ManCo innovation team; incumbents there actively scout RegTech.
- **Fit4Start**: €150k non-dilutive + coaching, 20 startups/cohort, explicitly favors
  digital/fintech — Kloer is a center-of-the-fairway applicant.
- **700+ startups** in the national ecosystem, government pushing fintech/regtech as a
  strategic pillar; Digital Tech Fund and LBAN active at pre-seed/seed.
- **Talent**: multilingual compliance professionals across the border region
  (230k+ daily cross-border commuters from FR/DE/BE) — domain-expert hiring pool
  no Silicon Valley RegTech can match.
