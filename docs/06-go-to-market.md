# Go-To-Market

## The GTM insight

Luxembourg's fund industry is a **20-square-kilometer market**. Everyone knows
everyone; ALFI events, LHoFT, and the Kirchberg lunch circuit are the actual
distribution channel. This kills US-style paid acquisition and rewards
founder-led, credibility-first selling. Plan accordingly.

## Phase 0 (months 0–3): credibility infrastructure

- Incorporate (see `08-legal-incorporation.md`), join **LHoFT** and **ALFI**.
- Apply to **Fit4Start** (digital category) — the grant matters less than the
  government-stamped legitimacy and the Luxinnovation network.
- Publish the **Luxembourg AML/CFT Obligations Index** — free, maintained,
  rulebook-as-code rendered as a public website. (US playbook: content moat + SEO;
  every RC in the country bookmarks it. This is our "Vanta's free security
  questionnaire" motion.)

## Phase 1 (months 3–9): design partners → first 10 customers

- **Target list**: 40 mid-market ManCos/AIFMs and 15 independent fund administrators —
  big enough to feel the pain (€200k+/yr compliance ops), small enough to lack
  in-house tooling. Below the top-20 that Finologee/KPMG serve.
- **Offer**: 3 design partners at 50% off Year 1 + white-glove onboarding in exchange
  for reference rights and case-study numbers ("€2,500 → €400 per file").
- **The demo**: live gap-scan of a (sanitized) real KYC file in under 5 minutes.
  One artifact, one wow.
- Founder-led sales only. Target: 10 paying by month 9.

## Phase 2 (months 9–18): channel + self-serve

- **Big-4/consultancy channel**: advisory teams doing KYC remediation adopt Kloer as
  their delivery tooling (they keep the client relationship, we get the subscription —
  the KPMG×Finologee 2025 deal proves the channel works; we offer the mid-market
  version with margin share).
- **Self-serve Starter tier** with public pricing — inbound from the Obligations
  Index + LinkedIn presence of founders (compliance officers live on LinkedIn).
- **Events**: ALFI Global Distribution Conference, ICT Spring, LHoFT demo days —
  speaking slots over booths.

## Phase 3 (months 18–30): expand

- **Ireland** (CBI rulebook module) — same buyer, English-speaking, doubles SAM.
- **Adjacent verticals in-country**: CAA-regulated insurers, VASP/crypto register
  entities, then the long tail of AML-obligated professionals (notaries, law firms) with
  a slimmed-down Starter.
