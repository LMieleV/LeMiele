# Business Model & Pricing

## Revenue engine 1 — SaaS subscription (core)

Priced per regulated entity, tiered by monitored-relationship volume and modules.

| Tier | Target customer | Includes | Price |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Starter** | Small PSF, single SARL-S/SARL entity, boutique AIFM | KYC Studio, 250 monitored relationships, obligation calendar | **€850/mo** (€10.2k/yr) |
| **Growth** | Mid-market ManCo / fund admin team | + Continuous Monitor, Evidence Room, 1,500 relationships, 5 seats | **€2,500/mo** (€30k/yr) |
| **Enterprise** | Multi-entity groups, ManCo platforms, large admins | + multi-entity console, SSO/SCIM, custom rulebooks, unlimited seats, DPA/22/806 pack | **from €6,000/mo** (€72k+/yr) |

Annual prepay standard (regulated buyers prefer it; helps working capital).

## Revenue engine 2 — usage margin (the Ramp/Middesk layer)

- Sanctions/PEP/adverse-media screening: pass-through + **30–40% margin** per check.
- ID&V (identity verification) per natural person: margin on provider cost.
- RCS/RBE registry retrievals bundled with markup.
- Expected to reach **15–20% of revenue by Year 3** at near-90% gross margin.

## Revenue engine 3 — marketplace & filings (Year 2+)

- Referral fees from RC-as-a-service firms, auditors, data vendors (10–15% of first-year
  contract value).
- Paid filing-preparation packs (annual AML/CFT survey, RC/RR reports).

## Why customers pay (the CFO math we sell with)

A mid-market ManCo doing ~300 KYC reviews/year at a blended €1,800 internal cost:
**€540k/year**. Kloer at €34k/year cutting per-file effort ~80% saves **≈ €400k/year**
— a 10×+ ROI story, Ramp-style savings positioning. The RC buys the audit trail; the
CFO buys this number.

## Pricing philosophy

- Anchor against consultants (€150–300/hr), not against software.
- Never price per-seat only — seats punish adoption; relationships monitored scale
  with customer value.
- Public pricing for Starter/Growth (self-serve trust signal, unusual in this market —
  a deliberate US-style pattern break that generates inbound).
