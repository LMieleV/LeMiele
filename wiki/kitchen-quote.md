# Kitchen Quote

## Summary
The kitchen is the top priority for the apartment work: we can't fully move in until it is installed. The decision is to request the kitchen quote **separately** from the rest of the apartment work, while telling suppliers that more work may follow. This page holds the reasoning, the quote-request email draft, and the details still needed to finish it.

## Key Points
- **Quote the kitchen separately.** A whole-apartment quote takes longer to prepare, and a general contractor may schedule the kitchen late in a bigger job. A kitchen-only request is faster and keeps the kitchen on the critical path.
- **Comparable quotes.** The same kitchen-only request can go to kitchen specialists and general contractors (2–3 suppliers), so prices compare like for like.
- **No lock-in.** The rest of the apartment work stays open to whichever contractor is best for it.
- **Keep the bundle lever.** The email mentions that further work may follow. With an itemised kitchen price in hand, a package discount can still be negotiated later.
- **Lead time matters as much as price.** The email asks for the earliest start date and the time from order to finished installation.
- **Interfaces to settle first:** if the kitchen floor is being replaced, it is usually laid before the cabinets go in. Plumbing and electrical points for the new layout belong in the kitchen quote, not in a later job.

## Email Draft

> **Subject:** Quote request – new kitchen at [address] (priority)
>
> Dear [Name],
>
> We would like a quote for a new kitchen in our apartment at [address]. The kitchen is our priority: we can't move in until it is installed, so the lead time matters to us as much as the price.
>
> **What we need**
> - Kitchen cabinets (base and wall units), [straight / L-shaped / U-shaped / with island] layout, approx. [X] m of wall
> - Countertop in [quartz / laminate / granite / other]
> - Sink and tap
> - Appliances: [hob, oven, extractor hood, fridge, dishwasher, microwave], built-in. Please quote with and without appliances.
> - Removal of the existing kitchen [if applicable]
> - Delivery and installation
> - Any plumbing and electrical changes needed for the new layout
>
> Attached are [the floor plan / measurements / photos].
>
> **Please include in your quote**
> 1. An itemised breakdown: cabinets, countertop, appliances, plumbing/electrical, delivery, installation
> 2. The lead time from order to finished installation, and your earliest available start date
> 3. Payment terms and warranty
> 4. Whether you need to visit to measure, and when you could come
>
> We would like the kitchen installed by [target date].
>
> We are also planning further work in the apartment[s] ([e.g. flooring, bathroom, painting]) and may ask you to quote for that separately later. For now, please price the kitchen on its own.
>
> Could you send the quote by [reply date]? We're happy to arrange a visit at a time that suits you.
>
> Best regards,
> [Your name]
> [Phone]
