# Kloer — Investor One-Pager

**Compliance, kloer a simpel.** AI-native continuous compliance for the €7-trillion
Luxembourg fund industry.

---

**The problem.** Luxembourg's ~3,000 regulated fund entities are legally required to
run AML/KYC programs that cost $1,500–$3,000 per client review and entire teams'
worth of manual work. The CSSF's 2026 supervisory priorities, DORA, and the incoming
EU AML Authority raise the bar again. Today's answer: spreadsheets and consultants at
€300/hour.

**The product.** Kloer connects to an entity's systems and (1) monitors its
obligations continuously against a rulebook-as-code library of CSSF/AML/DORA
requirements, (2) drafts KYC review files, risk assessments and regulatory reports
with evidence-linked AI, humans approving, and (3) keeps an always-current audit-proof
evidence room. KYC file cost drops ~6× (€2,500 → €400).

**The playbook.** Vanta proved compliance-automation-as-subscription ($2.45B).
Ramp proved savings-led workflow + take-rate expansion (5.5× volume in 2 years).
ServiceTitan proved that owning 100% of a "boring" vertical's workflow builds an
IPO-grade moat. Kloer recombines all three on the densest regulated market in Europe
— then repeats it in Dublin.

**Business model.** SaaS €850–€6,000+/month per entity + 30-40% margin on screening/
ID&V usage. 82% gross margin, <5-month CAC payback, NRR 115%+ target.

**Market.** SAM ~€55M/yr (Luxembourg fund industry) doubling with Ireland; TAM
~€120M/yr with the full AML-obligated long tail. Small, dense, non-discretionary —
and completely underserved below the enterprise tier.

**Competition.** Finologee (enterprise workflow, KPMG channel), KYC3 (screening
point solution), Governance.com (data governance) — none AI-native, none self-serve,
none continuous. US clones (Vanta/Drata) don't cover CSSF/AML at all.
